Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This is the consumer health data privacy policy of QuaintInc LLC (“Company”) for the EverPray application (the “App”). Washington’s My Health My Data Act and Nevada’s consumer health data privacy law require it. It applies to Washington and Nevada residents and to any person whose consumer health data the Company collects in Washington or Nevada. It covers only consumer health data under those laws. For all other information, see the EverPray Privacy Policy.

1. Consumer health data we collect, and why

The Company collects the categories of consumer health data below when you choose to share them, and uses each one only to provide the App to you:

  • Health information you include in a check-in, such as an illness, injury, treatment, pregnancy or mental health concern, for you or for someone you pray for. Used to write and safety-check your prayer. The App does not keep check-in answers after the prayer is written.
  • Health information that appears in your saved prayers, answered-prayer notes and prayer tags. Used to show your prayer history and, if you have an account, to sync and back it up.
  • Prayer Log notes the App’s AI writes from your check-in, which may describe a health need, such as “Healing for Mom after surgery.” Used to build your Prayer Log.

The Company also uses this data to maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App, and to comply with law.

2. Where the data comes from

The Company collects this consumer health data directly from you, and from the Prayer Log notes the App’s AI writes from what you share. The Company does not buy consumer health data and does not collect it from data brokers.

3. Consumer health data we share

The Company discloses the categories above only to service providers that process them on its behalf under contract, and only as needed for each one to perform its function. The Company does not sell consumer health data and will not sell it without your separate signed authorization. The Company does not share consumer health data for advertising and uses no advertising SDKs, ad networks, data brokers, or tracking pixels.

4. Who receives consumer health data

Categories of third parties: a cloud database and server provider, and an artificial intelligence service provider.

Specific recipients: Supabase (account storage, backup, and the server that sends check-ins for prayer writing) and OpenAI (writing prayers and Prayer Log notes, and safety checks).

Affiliates: None.

5. Your rights, and how to use them

You may ask the Company to confirm whether it collects your consumer health data, to access that data, to get a list of the third parties and affiliates that received it, to delete it, and to withdraw your consent to its collection. To make any request, email support@everprayapp.com. You can also delete your prayers, Prayer Log and prayer tags in Settings → Privacy → Delete all my prayers, or delete your account in Settings → Account. The Company will respond within 45 days, and may extend that once by 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice to you. The Company will not deny you the core App service for exercising these rights.

If the Company denies a request, you may appeal by replying to that decision, and the Company will tell you the result of the appeal. If you disagree with the outcome, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

6. Consent

Before your first prayer, the App asks your consent to process the health and other sensitive details you share and to send your check-ins to OpenAI to write and safety-check your prayers. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy, and the App then stops writing personal prayers for you. The Company discloses consumer health data only to its service providers as described above. It will ask for your separate consent before any other sharing or any new purpose, and for a separate signed authorization before any sale.
