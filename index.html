About

Why we created EverPray

We created EverPray to make daily prayer feel simpler, more personal, and easier to return to.

Sometimes we want to pray, but we’re not quite sure what to say. Or our prayers become rushed and general when there’s actually so much on our hearts. EverPray helps you slow down for a moment, reflect on what you’re carrying, and bring it honestly to God.

We also wanted to make it easier to weave Scripture and traditional prayer into everyday life, not as something separate, but as part of a simple daily rhythm.

And over time, EverPray helps you look back on the prayers God has answered. In the busyness of life, it can be easy to overlook the graces we’ve already received. Remembering them helps us practice gratitude, recognize God’s goodness, and grow in trust.

Our hope is that EverPray makes prayer feel less like one more thing to do, and more like a meaningful part of your day.

— Leah & Frank
