Privacy Policy

Your prayers are personal. Here’s how we care for them.

Effective November 9, 2026

EverPray is made by QuaintInc LLC (“QuaintInc,” “we,” “us”), a Texas limited liability company. This policy explains what information the EverPray app collects, how we use it, which service providers help us handle it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we use your information only to run EverPray for you. To do that, a few service providers process it for us: Supabase stores your account and backup, OpenAI writes your prayers and helps check them for safety, and RevenueCat and Apple handle subscriptions. We never sell your information, we never use it for advertising, and we don’t allow your prayers to be used to train AI models. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, or your Apple or Google sign-in, and a password if you create one. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider.
  • Guest use: if you use EverPray without an account, we create an anonymous account ID so the app can write prayers for you. A guest’s prayers stay only on their phone. Nothing is backed up unless you later create or sign in to an account and choose to keep those prayers.
  • Profile and preferences: the name you’d like to be called, gender (if you share it), faith tradition (Christian or Catholic), which prayer endings you want (for example a Bible verse or a prayer to the Blessed Mother), theme, appearance and text size.
  • Check-in answers: how you’re feeling, what’s on your heart, what you’re asking for, who you’re praying for, and anything you type or speak into them, the prayer length you choose, and any details you add when you ask for a prayer to be rewritten. We use these to write your prayer. EverPray doesn’t keep your check-in answers once the prayer is written.
  • Prayer tags: the people and intentions you choose to save as tags, with any details you add, so you can include them in future check-ins. Tags are kept until you edit or delete them.
  • Prayer history: the prayers EverPray writes for you (with any Scripture reference, Blessed Mother prayer or saint prayer that comes with them), your favorites, your Prayer Log, and any notes about answered prayers. Prayer Log entries are short summaries of your intentions that EverPray’s AI writes from your check-in, plus any you add yourself.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to support@everprayapp.com.

What you share may reveal sensitive information, such as your religious beliefs, your health or your family’s health, or other personal matters. We use it only to write and keep your prayers, as described in this policy.

Information collected automatically

  • Subscription status: whether you have EverPray Pro, a free trial or the free version, and which plan, through Apple and RevenueCat (described below). Apple processes the payment itself; we never see your card details.
  • Daily prayer count: how many personal prayers your account has received each day, so we can apply the free version’s daily allowance. For guests, we also count prayers per network to prevent abuse: we keep only a scrambled code made from your IP address (never the address itself), and regularly remove it after about two days. These counts never include what you wrote or what the prayer said.
  • Basic technical data: when the app connects to our service providers, they receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating system and app version, and keep service logs. Our own error logs record what went wrong, never the text of your check-ins or prayers.

EverPray does not include analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, uses subscription data to give us reports about subscriptions, such as how many people subscribe or renew (see “Who we share information with”).

Information that stays on your device

  • Voice input: when you tap “Speak instead,” your iPhone’s built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Depending on your device settings, Apple may process that audio under its own privacy policy. EverPray receives only the resulting text, never the audio.
  • Photos: EverPray asks for permission only to add the prayer images you choose to save. It does not read your photo library.
  • Reminders: reminders, including the optional daily Moment of thanks, are scheduled by your phone itself at the times you choose. Your reminder settings stay on your phone (and in your backup, if you have an account). Reminders never show your prayers, only a short note from EverPray. EverPray does not use push notifications from a server.

How we use your information

  • To write personal prayers from your check-in answers, prayer tags and preferences.
  • To check check-ins and prayers for safety, so EverPray can respond with care and show crisis resources when someone may be in danger.
  • To save, sync and back up your prayers, Prayer Log, prayer tags and settings, so they’re there when you change phones.
  • To run your account, subscription and free trial, and to apply the free version’s daily allowance.
  • To schedule the reminders you choose on your phone.
  • To keep EverPray secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to send account emails such as sign-in codes and password resets.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

AI-written prayers and safety checks

EverPray uses OpenAI to write prayers. When you create or rewrite a prayer, our server sends OpenAI the information needed to write it: your check-in answers and notes, any prayer tags you chose, any details you add, the name you’d like to be called, your gender if you’ve shared it, your faith tradition and prayer-ending preferences, and the length you chose. We don’t send your email address or account ID.

Safety checks. Before a prayer is written, your check-in goes through automated safety checks, including OpenAI’s moderation service, so EverPray can respond with care if something suggests you or someone else may be in danger. After the prayer is written, OpenAI also reviews the draft prayer before you see it, to check that it is safe and faithful to your tradition. These checks are automated: no person at EverPray reviews your check-in to write or check a prayer, and our server doesn’t keep your check-in afterwards.

Prayer Log. Along with each prayer, OpenAI writes one to three short notes of the intentions in your check-in (for example “Healing for Mom after surgery”). These are saved with the prayer for your Prayer Log.

How OpenAI handles this information:

  • No training. Under OpenAI’s API data policies, content sent through its API isn’t used to train or improve OpenAI’s models unless the API customer chooses to opt in. EverPray does not opt in, and has turned off data sharing in its OpenAI account.
  • Limited retention. We ask OpenAI not to store our requests as saved responses. OpenAI may still keep API inputs and outputs for a limited time to provide its service and to detect and prevent abuse. Its documentation currently describes this as up to 30 days. OpenAI may keep information longer when the law requires it.

We don’t allow any of our service providers to use your prayers to train AI models.

In the app, Settings → Privacy explains that your check-in is sent to OpenAI to write your prayer.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run EverPray, only as much as they need, and under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, secure cloud storage for your backup and daily prayer count, and the server that sends your check-in to OpenAI. Our Supabase project is hosted in the eastern United States.
  • OpenAI: writing prayers and Prayer Log notes, and safety checks, as described above.
  • RevenueCat: manages EverPray Pro subscriptions for us. RevenueCat receives a random customer ID (your EverPray account ID, or a separate random ID for guests). It also receives your subscription details from Apple: which plan you have, whether you’re in a free trial, purchase, renewal and expiration dates, and whether your subscription is active. Its software in the app also sends standard technical information such as device type, operating system, app version and IP address. RevenueCat uses this to confirm your subscription and to give us subscription reports and charts. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your account has EverPray Pro. We don’t send RevenueCat your name, email address, check-ins or prayers.
  • Resend: sends account emails for us, such as email confirmation and password-reset codes. Resend receives your email address and the content of those emails.
  • Apple: app distribution, Sign in with Apple if you choose it, speech recognition if you use “Speak instead,” and in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you choose it.
  • Our email provider: if you email us, your message is handled by our email provider, Proton Mail.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of EverPray users or others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In that case the new owner would remain bound by this policy.

How we protect your information

Information sent between the app and our service providers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Our cloud provider, Supabase, encrypts stored data at rest. Your backup is not end-to-end encrypted, which means our systems can read it in order to sync and restore it. On your phone, your data is protected by your device’s own security, such as your passcode. Our database rules let each account see only its own data. A small number of authorized administrators can technically access our systems, and we use two-step verification on our main service accounts. We don’t routinely look at your prayers or other content; we access account data only when needed to run, secure or fix EverPray, to respond to a request from you, or when the law requires it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

How long we keep it

  • Accounts: we keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, your account, backup and prayer counts are deleted from our live database right away, and we ask RevenueCat to delete its customer record. Copies in our database provider’s backups are removed as those backups expire, currently within about 7 days on our current plan, except where the law requires us to keep something longer (for example, purchase records).
  • “Delete all my prayers” removes your prayers, Prayer Log, answered prayers and prayer tags from your phone and, if you have an account, from your backup.
  • Guests: a guest’s prayers are stored only on their phone, and deleting the app deletes them. The anonymous account ID and its daily prayer counts stay on our systems until you delete them in Settings → Account → Delete my data, or ask us to. Deleting the app alone doesn’t remove them.
  • Service logs kept by our providers, such as Supabase’s request logs, are kept for a limited time under each provider’s settings (Supabase: currently about 7 days).
  • Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own retention practices. Account emails sent through Resend are kept under Resend’s practices. Information sent to OpenAI is kept only as described in the AI-written prayers section above.

Your choices and rights

  • Export and download your data: “Export my prayers” (in Settings and Settings → Account) and “Download my data” (in Settings → Privacy) are free for everyone, with or without EverPray Pro. Each creates a copy as a PDF, plain text file or spreadsheet (CSV). The copy includes:
    • every prayer saved in EverPray, with its date, title, text, favorite mark, and any Scripture reference, Blessed Mother prayer or saint prayer that came with it;
    • your Prayer Log, your answered prayers with your notes, and your prayer tags;
    • your name, account email (or “Guest”), plan, faith tradition, gender, prayer endings and reminder settings.
    It includes your full prayer history, even prayers you can’t browse in the app on the free version. It doesn’t include the daily prayer count, which you can ask us for.
  • Edit or delete Prayer Log items and prayer tags one at a time in the app (the Prayer Log is part of EverPray Pro). Individual prayers can’t currently be deleted one at a time. To remove your prayers, use Settings → Privacy → Delete all my prayers, which deletes all of your prayers, Prayer Log, answered prayers and prayer tags at once, on any plan.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account → Delete account (for guests, Delete my data), or email us. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
  • Reminders: turn them on or off in Settings → Notifications, or in your phone’s settings.

To use any of these rights, email support@everprayapp.com. We’ll verify your request and respond within the time the law allows, and we won’t treat you differently for making one. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our response; if you’re still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general. Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on performing our contract with you, your consent (which you can withdraw), and our legitimate interest in keeping EverPray safe and working.

Children

EverPray is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Where your information is stored

We are based in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. EverPray is currently offered only in the United States. If you use EverPray from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to the United States, with safeguards required by applicable law.
