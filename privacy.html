Privacy Policy

Your prayers are personal. Here’s how we care for them.

Effective October 18, 2026

EverPray is made by QuaintInc LLC (“QuaintInc,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what information the EverPray app collects, how we use it, which service providers help us handle it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we use your information only to run EverPray for you. To do that, a few service providers process it for us: Supabase stores your account and backup, OpenAI writes your prayers, and RevenueCat and Apple handle subscriptions. We never sell your information, we never use it for advertising, and we don’t allow your prayers to be used to train AI models. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, or your Apple or Google sign-in, and a password if you create one. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Guest use: if you use EverPray without an account, we create an anonymous account ID so the app can write prayers for you. A guest’s prayers stay only on their phone. Nothing is backed up unless you later create or sign in to an account and choose to keep those prayers.
  • Profile and preferences: the name you’d like to be called, gender (if you share it), faith tradition, which prayer endings you want (for example a Bible verse or a prayer to the Blessed Mother), theme and appearance.
  • Check-in answers: how you’re feeling, what’s on your heart, what you’re asking for, who you’re praying for, and anything you type or speak into them. We use these to write your prayer. EverPray doesn’t save your check-in answers once the prayer is written.
  • Prayer history: the prayers EverPray writes for you (with any Scripture reference, Blessed Mother prayer or saint prayer that comes with them), your favorites, the intentions in your Prayer Log, and any notes about answered prayers.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to support@everprayapp.com.

Information collected automatically

  • Subscription status: whether you have EverPray Pro, a free trial or the free version, and which plan, through Apple and RevenueCat (described below). Apple processes the payment itself; we never see your card details.
  • Daily prayer count: how many personal prayers your account has received each day, so we can apply the free version’s daily allowance. For guests, we also count prayers per network, to prevent abuse: we keep only a scrambled code made from your IP address (never the address itself), and delete it after two days. These counts never include what you wrote or what the prayer said.
  • Basic technical data: when the app connects to our service providers, they receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, operating system and app version, and keep service logs. Our own error logs record what went wrong, never the text of your check-ins or prayers.

EverPray does not include analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Information that stays on your device

  • Voice input: when you tap “Speak instead,” your iPhone or Android device’s built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Depending on your device settings, Apple or Google may process that audio under their own privacy policies. EverPray receives only the resulting text, never the audio.
  • Photos: EverPray asks for permission only to add the prayer images you choose to save. It does not read your photo library.
  • Reminders: reminders are scheduled by your phone itself, at the times you choose. Your reminder settings stay on your phone, and reminders never show your prayers, only a short note from EverPray.

How we use your information

  • To write personal prayers from your check-in answers and preferences.
  • To save, sync and back up your prayers, Prayer Log and settings, so they’re there when you change phones.
  • To run your account, subscription and free trial, and to apply the free version’s daily allowance.
  • To schedule the reminders you choose on your phone.
  • To keep EverPray secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

AI-written prayers

EverPray uses OpenAI to write prayers. When you create or regenerate a prayer, our server sends OpenAI the information needed to write it: your check-in answers and notes, the name you’d like to be called, your gender if you’ve shared it, your faith tradition and prayer-ending preferences, the length you chose, and any details you add. We don’t send your email address or account ID.

Before a prayer is written, your check-in also goes through automated safety checks, including OpenAI’s moderation service, so EverPray can respond with care if something suggests you or someone else may be in danger. These checks are automated. No one at EverPray reads your check-ins or prayers.

How OpenAI handles this information:

  • No training. Under OpenAI’s API data policies, content sent through its API isn’t used to train or improve OpenAI’s models unless the API customer chooses to opt in. EverPray does not opt in.
  • Limited retention. OpenAI may keep API inputs and outputs for a limited time to provide its service and to detect and prevent abuse. Its documentation currently describes this as up to 30 days. OpenAI may keep information longer when the law requires it.

We don’t allow any of our service providers to use your prayers to train AI models.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run EverPray, only as much as they need, and under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, secure cloud storage for your backup and daily prayer count, and the server that sends your check-in to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI: writing prayers and safety checks, as described above.
  • RevenueCat: manages EverPray Pro subscriptions for us. RevenueCat receives a random customer ID (your EverPray account ID, or a separate random ID for guests). It also receives your subscription details from Apple: which plan you have, whether you’re in a free trial, purchase, renewal and expiration dates, and whether your subscription is active. Its software in the app also sends standard technical information such as device type, operating system, app version and IP address. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your account has EverPray Pro. RevenueCat never receives your name, email address, check-ins or prayers.
  • Apple: app distribution, Sign in with Apple if you choose it, and in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you choose it.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of EverPray users or others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In that case the new owner would remain bound by this policy.

How we protect your information

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and, in our cloud storage, at rest. On your phone, it’s protected by your device’s own security, such as your passcode. Access is limited to what’s needed to run EverPray, and our database rules let each account see only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your prayers and personal information from our systems within 30 days. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer (for example, purchase records). Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are kept under their own retention practices. Information sent to OpenAI is kept only as described in the AI-written prayers section above.

A guest’s prayers are stored only on their phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Your choices and rights

  • Export and download your data: “Export my prayers” (in Settings) and “Download my data” (in Settings → Privacy) are free for everyone, with or without EverPray Pro. Each creates a copy as a PDF, plain text file or spreadsheet (CSV). The copy includes:
    • every prayer saved in EverPray, with its date, title, text, favorite mark, and any Scripture reference, Blessed Mother prayer or saint prayer that came with it;
    • your Prayer Log, and your answered prayers with your notes;
    • your name, account email (or “Guest”), plan, faith tradition, gender, prayer endings and reminder settings.
    It includes your full prayer history, even prayers you can’t browse in the app on the free version. It doesn’t include the daily prayer count, which you can ask us for.
  • Edit or delete individual items in the app at any time, or delete all your prayers in Settings → Privacy → Delete all my prayers.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account → Delete account, or email us.
  • Reminders: turn them on or off in Settings → Notifications, or in your phone’s settings.

Depending on where you live (including Texas, California, and the European Economic Area and UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to opt out of certain processing, and to appeal our decision on a request. To use any of these rights, email support@everprayapp.com. We’ll verify your request and respond within the time the law allows, and we won’t treat you differently for making one. Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on performing our contract with you, your consent (which you can withdraw), and our legitimate interest in keeping EverPray safe and working.

Children

EverPray is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Where your information is stored

We are based in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use EverPray from elsewhere, your information will be transferred there, with safeguards required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app before the changes take effect.
