Privacy Policy

What stays between you and God.

Effective September 29, 2026

PrayDay is made by QuaintInc LLC (“QuaintInc,” “we,” “us”). Your prayers are personal. This policy explains what information the PrayDay app collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version: we use your information only to run PrayDay for you. We never sell it, we never use it for advertising, and we don’t allow your prayers to be used to train AI models. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, or your Apple or Google sign-in, and a password if you create one. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Profile and preferences: the name you’d like to be called, gender, faith tradition, which prayer endings you want (for example a Bible verse or a prayer to the Blessed Mother), theme, appearance and reminder times.
  • Prayer content: your check-in answers (how you’re feeling, what’s on your heart, what you’re asking for, who you’re praying for) and anything you type or speak into them, the prayers PrayDay writes for you, your favorites, the intentions in your Prayer Log, and any notes about answered prayers.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to contact@praydayapp.com.

Information collected automatically

  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription or free trial, and which plan. Apple processes the payment itself; we never see your card details.
  • Basic technical data: the app version, device type and operating system, and error logs needed to keep PrayDay working.
  • Usage data, only if you allow it: if “Share anonymous usage data” is on in Settings → Privacy, we collect anonymous information about how features are used (for example, that a prayer was generated) to fix problems and improve PrayDay. It never includes the text of your prayers. You can turn it off at any time.

Information that stays on your device

  • Voice input: when you tap “Speak instead,” your iPhone or Android device’s built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Depending on your device settings, Apple or Google may process that audio under their own privacy policies. PrayDay receives only the resulting text, never the audio.
  • Photos: PrayDay asks for permission only to add the prayer images you choose to save. It does not read your photo library.
  • Face ID: if you turn on “Require Face ID to open,” your device handles the check. PrayDay never receives your biometric data.

How we use your information

  • To write personal prayers from your check-in answers and preferences.
  • To save, sync and back up your prayers, Prayer Log, reflections and settings, so they’re there when you change phones.
  • To run your account, subscription and free trial.
  • To send the reminders you choose.
  • To keep PrayDay secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

AI-written prayers

PrayDay uses OpenAI to write prayers. When you create or regenerate a prayer, we send OpenAI the information needed to write it: your check-in answers and notes, the name you’d like to be called, your faith tradition and prayer-ending preferences, the length you chose, and any details you add. We don’t send your email address or account details.

Under OpenAI’s API terms, content sent through its API is not used to train OpenAI’s models. OpenAI may keep it for a limited period to monitor for abuse, and then deletes it. We don’t allow any of our providers to use your prayers to train AI models.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run PrayDay, only as much as they need, and under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and secure cloud storage for your data.
  • OpenAI: writing prayers, as described above.
  • Apple (and Google on Android): app distribution, in-app purchases, subscriptions and push notifications.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of PrayDay users or others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In that case the new owner would remain bound by this policy.

How we protect your information

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is limited to what’s needed to run PrayDay, and our database rules let each account see only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your prayers and personal information from our systems within 30 days. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer (for example, purchase records).

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings → Export my prayers creates a copy of your prayers and Prayer Log as a PDF, text file or spreadsheet.
  • Edit or delete individual items in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Account → Delete account, or email us.
  • Usage data and notifications: turn these on or off in Settings.

Depending on where you live (including Texas, California, and the European Economic Area and UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to opt out of certain processing, and to appeal our decision on a request. To use any of these rights, email contact@praydayapp.com. We’ll verify your request and respond within the time the law allows, and we won’t treat you differently for making one. Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on performing our contract with you, your consent (which you can withdraw), and our legitimate interest in keeping PrayDay safe and working.

Children

PrayDay is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Where your information is stored

We are based in the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use PrayDay from elsewhere, your information will be transferred there, with safeguards required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app before the changes take effect.
